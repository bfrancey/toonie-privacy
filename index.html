Toonie Privacy Policy

Updated October 26, 2026

Toonie is a personal finance app for the Mac. In short: your financial data stays on your Mac, and Toonie doesn't collect it.

What Toonie stores, and where

The accounts, transactions, budgets and other details you enter or import are stored on your Mac, in Toonie's own folder. Toonie has no account to sign in to and no server, and it doesn't send your data anywhere.

What Toonie collects

Nothing. Toonie has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and shares nothing with third parties.

Backups

Toonie backs up your data on your Mac each day. If you choose a folder for copies of those backups (an external drive, for example), Toonie copies them there, locked with a password if you set one. Where that folder is, and who can reach it, is up to you.

Permissions Toonie may ask for

  • Calendar, only if you turn on Show in Calendar: Toonie adds your bill and payment dates to a calendar named Toonie, and changes only that calendar.
  • Notifications, only if you turn on reminders.
  • Touch ID or your Mac's password, only if you turn on the lock. macOS checks it; Toonie never sees it.
  • Files you choose: statements you import, and files you export or save.

Test versions

If you install Toonie through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. Toonie itself adds nothing to them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
